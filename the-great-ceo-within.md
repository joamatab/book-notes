
# Meetings
1. accountability (goals and actions)
    - last week's actions and goals. were they done?
        - if not, what blocked you?
        - what habit can you put in place to avoid this in the future?
    - next week. For each OKr, what action can dyou do to move them forward?
2. coaching 
 
Purpose: Help people become more effective, independent, and capable.

What is your biggest challenge right now?

What options have you considered?

What would you recommend if you were making the decision?

What skills or behaviors would you like to improve?

How can I help you grow?

Principle: Ask questions before giving advice. Help people develop their own solutions rather than solving every problem for them.
